Enrollment / Tuition

Enrollment at Acton Academy Kingwood is a Simple 3 Step Process:

1

Start by Learning More

Review the information on our website, particularly the materials on our Resources page.
Do as much research as you are able.

2

Schedule a Tour

Once you've done some research you'll want to schedule a campus tour.
You'll learn more about Acton Academy Kingwood and the campus and get an inside look at how the learning design works in real life.

3

Schedule Enrollment

If you think Acton Academy Kingwood is a good fit for your child and your family, the next step is to schedule your enrollment and lock in a spot.

You can start this process by clicking the "Start Your Journey" link at the top of the screen!

Tuition at Acton Academy Kingwood

Spark Studio (ages 4-7)

$9000/year

Payment plan as follows:

$900/month for 10 months

Payments start on August 1st and go through May 1st

Discovery Studio (ages 7-11) & Venture Studio (ages 11-14)

$9500/year

Payment plan as follows:

$950/month for 10 months

Payments start on August 1st and go through May 1st

There is an annual, non-refundable, $350 enrollment/material fee that covers all field trips, supplies, and materials (except laptops)
